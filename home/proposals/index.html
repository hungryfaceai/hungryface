Landing page proposals

Three directions for the Naptio home page. Each one works: buttons, Share and the paired / not-paired switch are live. Try them on your phone.

A · Clear & complete

A headline that says what Naptio is, one main button, a screenshot, then how it works, the four alerts, privacy and common questions.

Best for: new visitors from search, shares and the app stores. The conventional, best-converting shape.

B · One screen

Everything fits on one phone screen, like today’s page. First-time visitors see one “Get started” button; paired devices see Viewer, Camera and Pair another device.

Best for: people who already use Naptio and open it every night, including from the home-screen app.

C · Privacy first

Leads with what makes Naptio different: no cloud, no account, no subscription. A swipeable strip of app screens and a simple “where your video goes” picture.

Best for: standing out from cloud baby monitors, and parents who worry about privacy.

Round 2 · based on your feedback

New visitors: a shorter A, with privacy up front. Paired phones: a simple screen built around Viewer and Camera, with no pictures and only How it works, About and Share underneath. The device remembers which one you use and puts it first. “Contact us” opens a general enquiry form.

Each page has a new visitor · paired phone switch in its yellow banner, so you can see both screens on any device.

D · A, made shorter

New visitor: A cut to about half. Headline, one button, then “Private by design”, 3 steps and the alerts. Paired: “What is this device?” with two large buttons, Viewer and Camera.

Closest to what you described. Paired screen is the most explicit for a tired parent.

E · Privacy promise

New visitor: “The baby monitor that stays in your home” and a simple nursery → your phone picture with “No cloud. No account. No recordings.” Paired: one very large button for this device’s usual role, the other one smaller.

Strongest privacy message. Paired screen is a single tap most nights.

F · Show, then do

New visitor: everything in the first screen: headline, three short facts (private, smart alerts, 5 minutes) and one screenshot. Paired: an app-style home with two equal tiles and the room name.

Shortest new-visitor page. Paired screen feels most like an app.

Contact us · general enquiry form

Topic, optional name and message, then “Send”, which opens your email app with the message ready. No device details. Points people with a problem to the support form.

What competitors do

Hardware brands (Nanit, Owlet) lead with “shop now”, awards and reviews. Phone-based apps (Cloud Baby Monitor, BabyGuard, Dozi) lead with “turn two phones into a monitor” and privacy: “no cloud”, “no account”, “end-to-end encrypted”. Naptio’s edge is that it is free and works in the browser, so all three lead with privacy and “free”, not price or hardware.
