How it works

Two devices, one private link between them. Nothing to buy and nothing to install.

  1. Pair your two devices

    Scan a QR code or send a link. This links them, so only your devices can connect to each other.

  2. Start the Camera

    Put a spare phone or tablet near the cot, plugged in, with a clear view of your baby.

  3. Watch from your phone

    See and hear your baby on the Viewer, and get an alert when something needs your attention.

Alerts that help you rest

Private by design

Naptio was built by parents who didn’t want their baby’s bedroom on someone else’s server.

Read the privacy notice
  • No cloud video. Video and sound go directly between your devices. We never record or store them.
  • No account. No email, no password, no sign-up.
  • Your data stays with you. Alert history and settings stay on your device, and you can erase them any time.
  • No tracking cookies. We count visits without cookies and without storing your IP address.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Naptio is free for personal use. There’s no subscription and no paid version of the features above.

What do I need?

Two devices, such as a spare phone or tablet for the baby’s room and your own phone, on the same Wi-Fi. Keep the Camera plugged in, as it stays on all night.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Naptio works in your web browser. You can also add it to your home screen, or get it from the App Store or Google Play.

Can anyone else see my baby?

Only devices you have paired. Pairing creates a private code, and the link you share to pair should be kept private, like a password.

Does it replace watching my baby?

No. Naptio is not a medical device and can miss things. Always follow safe-sleep advice and keep an adult responsible for your baby.
