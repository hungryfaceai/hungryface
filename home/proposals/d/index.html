A private baby monitor from two phones you already own

Watch your baby from your own phone and get alerts for crying, movement and face-down sleeping. The video never leaves your devices.

Get started, it’s free How it works

Private by design

  • No cloud video. Video and sound go straight from one phone to the other. We never see or store them.
  • No account. No email, no password, no sign-up.
  • Stays on your device. Alert history and settings never leave it.
  • Only your devices. A private code links your two phones; no one else can connect.

Ready in about 5 minutes

  1. Pair two devices

    Scan a QR code. You do this once.

  2. Start the Camera

    A spare phone near the cot, plugged in.

  3. Watch from your phone

    See, hear and get alerts.

Alerts for

  • Crying
  • Movement
  • Leaving the cot
  • Face-down sleeping
