Help

Naptio turns two phones or tablets into a baby monitor. One watches your baby, the other shows you the video and alerts you. Video stays between your two devices; it isn’t uploaded anywhere.

Naptio is not a medical device. It can miss things. Always follow safe-sleep advice and keep an adult responsible for your baby.

Set up in 3 steps

Takes about 5 minutes. You only pair once.

You’ll need:
  • Two devices: a spare phone or tablet for the baby’s room (the Camera) and your own phone (the Viewer).
  • The same Wi-Fi on both devices.
  • A charger for the Camera, as it stays on all night.

  1. Pair your two devices

    This links them, so only your devices can see your baby.

    1. On the Camera device, open Pair devices and tap Share.
    2. Send the link to your Viewer phone (by message or email), or scan the QR code with it using Scan QR.
    3. Open the link on the Viewer. A green Device paired message confirms it. That’s it.

    Keep that link private, like a password. Anyone who has it could view your camera.

  2. Start the Camera

    1. On the Camera device, open Camera (Baby).
    2. Tap Start streaming, and allow camera and microphone access when asked.
    3. Place it where it can see the cot clearly, out of the baby’s reach, and plug it in.

    The screen stays on by itself while streaming. Cables must be well away from the cot.

  3. Watch on your phone

    1. On your Viewer phone, open Viewer (Parent). The video appears in a few seconds.
    2. Tap Enable audio to hear the room.
All set! Next time, just do steps 2 and 3. You don’t need to pair again.

Everyday use

  • Switch screens with the menu button (☰) at the top left.
  • Listen: Enable audio / Disable audio.
  • Talk to your baby: hold Talk; your voice plays on the Camera.
  • Play a lullaby on the Camera: tap Lullaby.
  • Full screen for night-time: tap Fullscreen.
  • See past alerts: open Dashboard from the menu.

Extra features optional

Naptio can alert you when something happens. Each one works best after a short set-up. Open the one you want from the menu; each screen has its own Help button with more detail.

Crying alertsGet alerted when your baby cries
  1. Open Audio from the menu and tap Start.
  2. For best results, first let it learn what a quiet room sounds like: use Settings on that screen with the room silent for about 20 seconds.

Getting too many or too few alerts? AI training teaches it the sounds of your home.

Movement alertsGet alerted when your baby moves or wakes
  1. Open Motion from the menu.
  2. While your baby is still, tap Calibration, then Start. This teaches it what “still” looks like.

Moved the camera? Run Calibration again.

Face-down alertsGet alerted if your baby may be lying face-down
  1. Open Prone from the menu.
  2. With your baby lying on their back, open Settings and run the calibration for a few seconds.

This is an extra prompt only. It can’t replace safe-sleep practice: always lay your baby on their back to sleep.

Zone alertsGet alerted when there’s movement in one area
  1. Open Fence from the menu and tap Draw zone.
  2. Tap around the area to watch, such as inside the cot, to draw its outline.

Tap Clear to start again. Redraw it if you move the camera.

Alerts & historySee and review what happened
  • A red banner at the top shows a new alert.
  • The alerts button shows recent ones; the number is how many are new.
  • The Dashboard shows a timeline you can filter and export.

Alert history is kept only on this phone.

Fix a problem

I can’t see the video
  1. Check the Camera still says it’s streaming. If not, tap Start streaming again.
  2. Make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi. Turning Wi-Fi off and on again often helps.
  3. If you use a VPN, turn it off on both devices.
  4. Still nothing? Pair the devices again (step 1).
I can’t hear anything
  • Tap Enable audio. Phones only play sound after you tap the screen.
  • Check your phone isn’t on silent and the volume is up.
  • On the Camera, check Audio ON is ticked and the microphone was allowed.
It asks me to pair again

This device doesn’t have your pairing link, for example after clearing the browser or using a different browser. Repeat step 1.

Pairing is stored per browser, so use the same browser each time.

The camera or microphone is blocked
  • iPhone/iPad: Settings → Safari (or your browser) → Camera / Microphone → Allow.
  • Android: tap the icon left of the web address → Permissions → allow Camera and Microphone.
  • Then reload the page.
The Camera stopped during the night
  • Keep it plugged in and turn off battery saver or low-power mode.
  • Leave Naptio open on screen; don’t switch to other apps on the Camera.
Too many alerts, or alerts missed
  • Run the set-up for that alert again (see Extra features), especially after moving the camera.
  • Each alert screen has a Help button with tips on adjusting it.
Start again from scratch

Open Settings and use Factory reset. It clears everything Naptio stored on this device only: pairing, alerts and settings. Then pair again.

Privacy & safety

  • Private: video and sound go directly between your two devices and are encrypted. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
  • Your devices only: only devices you paired can connect.
  • Not a medical device: it’s an aid, not a replacement for supervision or safe-sleep practice.
  • Consent: if you monitor anyone other than your own child, ask them first and follow local laws.

More detail in our Privacy Notice.

For advanced users: room names and changing the pairing link
  • Rooms: on Pair devices, a Room name (e.g. “Baby”) keeps separate cameras apart. Use one per child or space, and choose it on the Viewer in Settings.
  • New link: Rotate secret makes a new pairing link and stops old ones working; pair your devices again afterwards. Do this if a link was shared by mistake.
  • Remove a device: Forget on this device unpairs just that device.
