Not a medical device. Prone detection can miss a face-down position or raise false alerts. It doesn’t replace safe-sleep practice: always put your baby to sleep on their back, and keep an adult responsible for your baby.

What it does

Prone detection watches your baby’s face on the Camera and alerts you if your baby may be lying face down. It uses two independent checks. Either one can raise the alert:

  • Head tilt: the head is tipped forward much more than when your baby lies on their back.
  • Face hidden: the face can no longer be seen, for example face down in the mattress or covered by a blanket.

The video is analysed on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

Set up (about 1 minute)

  1. Place the Camera above or at the side of the cot, so your baby’s face is clearly in view. Good, even light helps.
  2. Calibrate while your baby is lying calmly on their back: open Settings, then under Calibration (supine baseline) press Start and wait for the timer (10 seconds by default). This saves how the head looks when your baby is face up. It is saved for this room.
  3. Leave the other settings on their defaults. Adjust them only if you get false alerts or missed ones (see Fix a problem).

Calibrate again if you move the Camera or change the room.

Reading the screen

Risk barHow likely a face-down position is right now, from 0 to 100%. It shows the higher of the two checks (head tilt or face hidden), smoothed over a couple of seconds. Green below 50%, orange from 50%, red at 100%.
Tick marksThe levels at which the head-tilt check starts and clears an alert.
AlertShown, with a sound if enabled in Settings, only once the risk has stayed high for a set time. It clears once the risk has stayed low for a set time. This avoids alerts from a brief turn of the head.
Face markersDots on the face and an arrow showing which way the head points. They confirm the face is being tracked.

How it works

Face tracking. Each video frame goes through Google’s MediaPipe Face Landmarker, which finds the face and estimates the head’s angles. Pitch is the forward/back tilt.

Head-tilt check. The pitch is compared with the face-up baseline from calibration. The instant risk starts rising 10° before the head reaches Pitch θ on and is at its maximum 20° past it. The risk is smoothed (EMA τ). An alert starts when it stays above Pose thr on for Pose dwell on seconds, and clears when it stays below Pose thr off for Pose dwell off seconds.

Face-hidden check. Each frame counts as 1 when no face is found and 0 when it is. That value is smoothed the same way. An alert starts when it stays above Occlusion thr on for Occlusion dwell on seconds, and clears below Occlusion thr off after Occlusion dwell off seconds.

Two thresholds and two waits (“hysteresis”) stop the alert flickering on and off around a single value. The alert cause (head tilt, face hidden, or both) is recorded in the Dashboard.
