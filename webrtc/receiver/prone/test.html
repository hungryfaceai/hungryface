Goal. Detect sustained face-down (prone) risk by fusing face visibility and head pitch from MediaPipe Face Landmarker.

Calibrate supine. With the baby face-up, press Start calibration for a few seconds. We store the average pitch as baseline.

Pitch θon. Degrees below supine to consider “face-down”. Increase if you get false positives.

EMA τ. Smooths noisy pitch/risk over time. Larger = smoother, slower.

Hysteresis. Use thr on/off and dwell to require sustained risk before alerting.

Not a medical device. Use as an assistive cue.
