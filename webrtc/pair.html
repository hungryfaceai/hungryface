Pair once, then connect securely without room codes. This exchanges device public keys and lets you verify with a short code (SAS).

Open this page on the other device by scanning the QR with its camera. It will land here with a secure pairing payload.

What you'll see

  1. Both devices display the same Short Authentication String (SAS).
  2. Tap Accept on both devices if the codes match.
  3. The device is saved under Trusted devices.
