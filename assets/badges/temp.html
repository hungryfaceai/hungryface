Install Naptio on your iPhone

No App Store download: install via “Add to Home Screen”.
  1. 1
    On your iPhone or iPad, open Safari and go to Naptio’s main page . URL: https://hungryfaceai.github.io/hungryface/home/ If you’re seeing these instructions inside another app (Mail, Messages, social, etc.), first open Naptio’s main page in Safari.
  2. 2
    In Safari, tap the Share icon in the toolbar (at the bottom of the screen on iPhone, top right on iPad).
  3. 3
    In the sheet that opens, scroll if needed and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. 4
    Confirm the name (Naptio) and tap Add.
    You’ll now see the Naptio icon on your Home Screen and can open it just like any app.

Tip: For the best experience, always launch Naptio from the Home Screen icon rather than from a browser tab.

Using Android instead? You can install Naptio as an Android app from Google Play .
