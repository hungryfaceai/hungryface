About Naptio

What is Naptio?

Naptio turns two devices you already own into a private baby monitor. One device is the Camera (Sender) near your baby; your other device is the Viewer. Video and audio travel directly between your devices using WebRTC, and simple on-device AI helps surface helpful cues—like cry-like sounds, motion, face orientation, and prone (face-down) risk.

There’s no cloud video storage. Streams stay local to your devices.

What you need

  • Two devices (e.g., two phones, or a phone + a laptop).
  • Reliable Wi-Fi or the same local network for both devices.
  • A safe place to mount the Camera device near the crib/cot, with power if possible.
  • Updated browsers (recent Safari/Chrome/Edge) with camera & mic permissions allowed.

How it works

After pairing, the Camera and Viewer connect directly using WebRTC. The AI runs on your devices: audio classification for cry-like sounds, motion cues, face landmarks, and prone-risk detection. Alerts appear locally in the app (banner + drawer). You can review recent alerts in the Alerts Dashboard.

  • Local-only by design: No cloud video storage. No third-party analytics for video.
  • Pairing options: quick room code (PSK) or a one-time trusted “fingerprint” flow.
  • Multiple Viewer pages: Video, Audio (cry detection), Motion, Face/Prone, Fence.
  • Alert history: Stored locally on your device for easy review.

Privacy & security

Naptio aims to keep your family’s data on your devices. Streams are peer-to-peer where possible, and AI runs locally. Minimal technical telemetry may be collected (e.g., feature usage) to improve reliability, with retention policies in place. See the Legal, Terms of Use & Privacy Notice for details.

Getting started in a minute

  1. On the device near the crib, open Camera (Sender) and allow camera/mic.
  2. On your other device, open a Viewer page.
  3. Use Pair devices to connect them (room code or fingerprint flow).
  4. Pick the detection page(s) you want and do a quick test alert.

Tips for a smooth setup

  • Keep the Camera device plugged in to avoid sleep/low-power interruptions.
  • Allow autoplay/sound (Safari/iOS) if you want instant audio on the Viewer.
  • Place the Camera device safely—stable surface, good view, away from the cot/crib interior.
  • Use the Help page for detailed pairing and troubleshooting steps.
